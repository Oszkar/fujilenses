# Laowa audit — 2026-11-21

Scope: the seven native Fujifilm X still-photo lenses requested for addition. Brand is **Laowa** (manufacturer Venus Optics). All sources below were checked on **2026-11-21**. These are new entries, not corrections of existing records. Prices and retailer URLs remain the reserved `0`, `0`, and empty-string values.

## Proposed catalog values

All entries use `mountType: XF`, `autofocus: false`, `linearMotor: false`, `imageStabilization: false`, and `apertureRing: true`. The focus/motor negatives follow the documented fully manual designs. Stabilization and weather evidence is distinguished below; silence in a specification table is not evidence of absence.

The two zooms require `filterViaAdapter: true`: their filter sizes refer to supplied removable front hood/adapter attachments, not bare-lens front threads. The 12–24mm is the **APS-C Zoom Shift CF**, not the older full-frame non-shift 12–24mm. The 25mm is **CF**, not the separate MFT Argus 25mm. `maxAperture` records the wide-end maximum for variable-aperture zooms; the full range stays in the model name.

## Per-lens evidence and limitations

### 8–16mm F3.5–5 Zoom CF

- [Manufacturer product page](https://www.venuslens.net/product/laowa-8-16mm-f3-5-5-zoom-cf/) specifies native X, focal/aperture range, 463 g, 86 mm, manual focus, and a de-clickable aperture ring.
- [Manufacturer manual](https://www.laowalens.com/Public/Uploads/uploadfile/files/20240403/CF8-16mmF3.5-5.0C-Dreamer.pdf), English specifications, explicitly excludes hood and both caps from approximately 463 g. It gives one nominal value for multiple mounts; it is not a separately measured X sample. [Official Japanese product table](https://www.laowa.jp/cat1/cab1e6580bc03c9b80d8dc87fb55be1e0eb65b48.html) repeats that basis and cautions that dimensions/weight vary by mount.
- [Exact X retailer listing, VE816FX](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/1790144-REG/venus_optics_ve816fx_laowa_8_16mm_f_3_5_5_zoom.html) corroborates the nominal weight (462.66 g converted from 1.02 lb) and explicitly specifies no image stabilization. Use manufacturer-rounded 463 g, not a falsely precise pound conversion.
- [Firsthand review](https://phillipreeve.net/blog/review-laowa-cf-8-16mm-f-3-5-5-0-zoom-aps-c/) describes the supplied click-on filter adapter and no weather sealing. Tested mount is Nikon Z: construction evidence only, not X weight evidence. [Exact X retailer](https://www.foto-erhardt.com/lenses/laowa-lenses/laowa-8-16mm-f3-5-5-zoom-for-fuji-x.html) independently identifies 86 mm filters on the hood. B&H lists the hood in the box.
- [Manufacturer launch video description, 2023-10-10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ltZ_mxkJVs) includes Fujifilm X; Japanese availability was November 24, 2023. Video description read; video not watched.

### 9mm F2.8 Zero-D

- [Manufacturer specifications](https://www.venuslens.net/product/9mm/) list native X, 9mm F2.8, nominal 215 g and native 49 mm filter thread. Weight is a shared manufacturer nominal specification, with caps/hood inclusion unspecified.
- [Firsthand Fujifilm X review](https://www.5050travelog.com/camera-and-lens-reviews/laowa-9mm-f28-review) confirms the clicked physical aperture ring, manual focus, 215 g/49 mm and early-2018 release.
- [X-T3 firsthand review](https://mirrorlesscomparison.com/stories/laowa-9mm-f-2-8-zero-d-fuji-x-barcelona/) explicitly reports no weather sealing. Water-repellent front-element coating does not establish weather sealing. [X-T3 review](https://www.photographyblog.com/reviews/laowa_9mm_f2_8_zero_d_review) is supplementary construction evidence.
- User ASIN **B07BHQHKCF** is associated with X SKU **VE928FX** by [retailer identifier evidence](https://www.manor.ch/de/p/mp-14bd0f1b-tproduct). Amazon itself was inaccessible; this identity is not based on an opened Amazon product page.

### 10mm F4 Cookie

- [Manufacturer specifications](https://www.venuslens.net/product/laowa-10mm-f-4-cookie/) list native X, manual focus, 10mm F4, nominal 130 g and a direct 37 mm filter thread. The source gives a shared manufacturer nominal weight; caps/hood inclusion and a separately measured X weight are not specified.
- [Firsthand review](https://petapixel.com/2022/07/26/laowa-10mm-f-4-cookie-lens-review-great-glass-with-lackluster-usability/) describes the clicked physical aperture ring and absence of weather/dust sealing. [Supplementary hands-on review](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/reviews/laowa-10mm-f4-cookie-lens-review) describes the lack of electronic contacts; no powered stabilization follows that mechanical design.
- [Manufacturer launch description, 2022-07-19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i_2Cj-Eetz0) establishes 2022 availability. Description read; video not watched.
- User [Amazon ASIN B0B7BPZDKM](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B0B7BPZDKM) was confirmed by its browser page title as the **10mm F4 Cookie for Fuji X, black**. This is an identity check; use the manufacturer for specifications.

### 12–24mm F5.6 Zoom Shift CF

- [Manufacturer product/specification page](https://www.venuslens.net/product/laowa-12-24mm-f-5-6-zoom-shift-cf/) lists X, manual focus, 12–24mm F5.6, 575 g and 77 mm filters **with the hood attached**. It specifies ±7 mm shift. [Exact X retailer VE1224S-FX](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/1860955-REG/venus_optics_ve1224s_fx_laowa_12_24mm_f_5_6_zoom.html) agrees on 575 g and includes the hood.
- [Manufacturer manual](https://www.laowalens.com.cn/Public/Uploads/uploadfile/files/20250825/LAOWACF12-24mmF5.6ZoomShiftguangjiaobianjiaoyizhoujingtoushuomingshu.pdf) labels the physical aperture ring and filter connection ring and explains manual aperture operation without CPU contacts. No powered stabilization is inferred from that fully mechanical, contactless design.
- **Unresolved weight conflict:** the manual specifies approximately **604 g excluding front/rear caps**, while the current global product page and exact-X retailer specify **575 g** without an accessory basis. Keep 575 g as the current X catalog nominal value, qualified by this conflict. Do not average the values, claim 575 g is measured bare-X weight, or assume the 29 g difference is the hood without evidence.
- [Dan Garner's firsthand Fujifilm X-T5 review](https://www.photo-odyssey.com/gear-reviews/laowa-zoom-shift-review) explicitly states in its FAQ that the lens is not weather sealed, supporting `weatherResistant: false`. It also confirms no electronics, the physical aperture ring and the included hood's role as a 77 mm filter adapter. The retail front-element coating claim alone would not establish weather sealing.
- [Manufacturer launch description, 2024-11-05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hN3qrrNeq9Y) and [official Japanese announcement](https://www.laowa.jp/news/2024/12/laowa-12-24mm-f56-zoom-shift-cf.html) establish 2024 availability (Japan December 13). Video description read; video not watched.

### Argus 25mm F0.95 CF APO

- [Manufacturer specifications](https://www.venuslens.net/product/laowa-argus-25mm-f-0-95-apsc-apo/) list 25mm F0.95, X mount, manual focus, 575 g, native 62 mm thread and an aperture-ring click switch. [Exact X retailer VE2595FX](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/1733767-REG/venus_optics_ve2595fx_argus_25mm_f_0_95_cf.html) agrees and explicitly specifies no stabilization.
- 575 g is manufacturer nominal, corroborated for the X listing; caps/hood inclusion is unspecified. It is not a measured X sample.
- [Firsthand Nikon Z review](https://phillipreeve.net/blog/review-laowa-argus-25mm-f-0-95-cf-apo-aps-c/) explicitly describes no stabilization, no electronic contacts and no mount sealing gasket. Used for shared construction only, not X weight. This supports `weatherResistant: false` with that mount qualification.
- [Manufacturer video description, 2022-11-24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CoXtQPnXVA) lists X among the available mounts, establishing 2022; do not confuse the later Japanese release with worldwide availability. Video description read; video not watched.

### Argus 33mm F0.95 CF APO

- User [Amazon ASIN B094393JNC](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B094393JNC) was confirmed by its browser page title as the **Argus 33mm F0.95 CF APO for Fuji X, black**. This is an identity check, not specification evidence.
- [Manufacturer specifications and ring description](https://www.venuslens.net/product/laowa-argus-33mm-f-0-95-cf-apo/) specify 33mm F0.95, native X, manual focus, 590 g, native 62 mm thread and a stepless aperture ring. [Exact X retailer](https://www.parkcameras.com/shop/laowa-argus-33mm-f095-cf-apo-lens-for-fujifilm-x_l242901f) corroborates 590 g and the ring.
- [Official Japanese table](https://www.laowa.jp/cat1/7839ebf57335d77e528f1089b52dc0d82a84f429.html) qualifies weight as mount-dependent. Thus 590 g is an X-retailer-corroborated manufacturer nominal, not a separately verified bare-X measurement; caps/hood inclusion remains unspecified.
- [Firsthand review](https://www.slrlounge.com/venus-optics-laowa-argus-33mm-f095-review/) explicitly reports no weather sealing or electronics; absence of powered stabilization follows the fully mechanical design. [Exact X listing](https://camera-tweedehands.nl/en/laowa-argus-33mm-f095-cf-apo-fujifilm-x) explicitly says no OIS.
- [Contemporary launch report, 2021-05-03](https://www.dpreview.com/news/4470142107/venus-optics-launches-laowa-argus-33mm-f0-95-cf-apo-lens-for-aps-c-camera-systems/) reports X availability. This complements the current manufacturer specifications; it is launch reporting, not a new measurement.

# Released catalog additions

Checked 2026-10-31. Five non-tilt Fujifilm X models added; price fields remain unused placeholders. Tilt lenses are intentionally deferred because their movements and characteristics need separate modeling. Announced Sirui Swift 20/40 and Tamron B091 X versions remain excluded until shipping.

| Slug | Applied specifications and evidence |
|---|---|
| `xf-400mm-f45-lm-ois-wr` | 400mm F4.5 prime; AF, linear motor, OIS and WR confirmed by [Fujifilm launch announcement](https://www.fujifilm.com/au/en/news/hq/13872). Released September 2026. [Official specifications](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/products/lenses/xf400mmf45-r-lm-ois-wr/specifications/) give 1245g and 95mm filters. The shared XF500/XF400 manual already audited in [fujinon.md](fujinon.md) confirms physical aperture ring and weight excluding caps, hood and tripod foot. |
| `sigma-50-120mm-f28` | 50–120mm F2.8 zoom; [official X specifications](https://www.sigma-global.com/en/lenses/c026_50_120_28/) give 390g and 55mm filters (accessory exclusion basis not specified). [Sigma announcement](https://press.sigmaphoto.com/product/10/sigma-announces-50-120mm-f2-8-dc-os-contemporary/) explicitly confirms dedicated aperture ring, linear AF, optical stabilization, dust/splash resistance and October 22, 2026 availability. |
| `viltrox-af-75mm-f18-evo` | 75mm F1.8 AF prime, 325g, 58mm filter, STM + lead screw (not linear motor), mount sealing, from [official X specifications](https://viltrox.com/products/af-75mm-f1-8-xf). Its generic marketing paragraph says 355g, but the X specification table says 325g; [Bic Camera mount comparison](https://www.biccamera.com/bc/item/15210827/) corroborates X=325/E=335/Z=355. Accessory exclusion basis unspecified. Physical clicked aperture ring; X lacks the E/Z de-click switch. [Hands-on review](https://www.the-digital-picture.com/Reviews/Viltrox-75mm-F1-8-EVO-Lens.aspx) confirms no optical stabilization. |
| `viltrox-af-90mm-f22-evo` | 90mm F2.2 AF prime, 325g, 58mm filter, STM + lead screw, mount sealing from [official X specifications](https://viltrox.com/products/af-90mm-f2-2-xf). [X-mount hands-on review](https://dustinabbott.net/2026/06/viltrox-evo-90mm-f2-2-x-mount-review/) confirms clicked aperture ring, seal points, 325g and no stabilization. Accessory exclusion basis unspecified. Both EVO models launched June 2026: [official launch article](https://viltrox.com/blogs/new-in/new-evo-series-75mm-and-90mm-aps-c-primes). |
| `ttartisan-af-35mm-f18` | Original generation, separate from II. [Manufacturer manual](https://ttartisan.com/static/upload/file/20240219/1708329488943072.pdf) specifies E/X, 35mm F1.8 AF, 199g and 52mm filters; accessory exclusion basis unspecified. [Distributor announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000320.000070183.html) dates X release November 20, 2023. [X-H2 hands-on review](https://dustinabbott.net/2023/11/ttartisan-af-35mm-f1-8-review/) explicitly confirms no aperture ring and STM (linearMotor=false). [X-specific secondary specifications](https://cameradecision.com/lenses/specs/TTArtisan-AF-35mm-F1.8-X-Mount) support no optical stabilization or weather sealing; these negatives are not explicit manufacturer claims. |

Weather resistance on the EVO entries reflects documented sealing, not a waterproof rating. No weights were averaged across mounts.

# Fujinon lens data audit

Checked 2026-10-31 against `src/lib/data/xf-lenses.json` (48 records). The two sourced weight corrections below are applied to the JSON.

## Scope and result

All 48 weights and physical-aperture-ring booleans were cross-referenced against Fujifilm's own lens manuals. **All 48 current weights match after correcting two entries. All 48 aperture-ring values are supported.** “Supported absent” means the manufacturer manual's complete external-parts list has only focus/zoom controls, with camera-controlled aperture where described; it is not a conclusion from a missing “R” in the product name. Positive ring evidence is the manual explicitly labeling the physical aperture ring.

This is **not an all-fields certification**. Fields not systematically revalidated here: release year, autofocus, linear motor, weather resistance, stabilization, focal lengths, maximum aperture, filter diameter, reviews and commercial fields. Manuals supply many of those specifications, but a source existing is different from that field having been individually checked. Prices of zero and blank retailer URLs are existing placeholders, not sourced price claims. Catalog completeness was only spot-checked.

Sources below are retrieved manufacturer PDFs, discovered via the [official manual index](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/support/manual/lenses/). Page numbers are physical PDF pages (one-based), not printed EN page labels. Shared manuals contain several models; the row's weight is the column for that exact model, not a shared or averaged figure.

## Corrections and qualifications

- **XC 13-33mm F3.5-6.3 OIS:** `weightGrams` 130 → **125** (applied). Both the manual (PDF p16) and [official product specifications](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/products/lenses/xc13-33mmf35-63-ois/specifications/) say 125 g without cap/hood.
- **XC 15-45mm F3.5-5.6 OIS PZ:** `weightGrams` 136 → **135** (applied). Both the manual (PDF p16) and [official product specifications](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/products/lenses/xc15-45mmf35-56-ois-pz/specifications/) say 135 g excluding caps. Do not derive grams by converting rounded ounces.
- **XC 13-33 releaseYear 2026 needs a defined policy, not a blind correction.** Fujifilm [announced it on October 23, 2025 and specified standalone North American availability in late January 2026](https://newsroom-us.fujifilm.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Small-Wonder-Fujifilm-Introduces-FUJINON-XC13-33mmF3-5-6-3-OIS-10-23-2025/default.aspx). Its [New Zealand announcement](https://www.fujifilm.com/nz/en/news/hq/13019) describes an X-T30 III kit from December 2025. Announcement year, first kit availability, and standalone sale year differ. Keep 2026 only if standalone availability is the intended definition.
- **Catalog omission resolved:** [XF400mmF4.5 R LM OIS WR](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/products/lenses/xf400mmf45-r-lm-ois-wr/) was added after this 48-record audit; see [addition evidence](catalog-additions.md). The shared XF500/XF400 manual below specifies 1245 g for the XF400 (excluding caps, hood and tripod foot), a physical aperture ring, and 95 mm filter.
- **Weight basis matters even without mount variants.** The manuals explicitly exclude tripod collar feet for XF50-140 (p20), XF100-400 (p20), XF150-600 (p24), and XF500 (p22). Their current weights match those bases. XF200's manual p30 lists 2265 g excluding caps/hoods and separately lists the bundled teleconverter as 130 g; 2265 is not the total kit weight. Some product-page footnotes omit the tripod-foot exclusion found in the manual. Preserve the more specific manual qualification.
- These are Fujifilm X-mount products, so no Canon/Sony weight averaging is needed. This audit does not establish whether any historical data entry was averaged; it establishes that the current values, after the two corrections above, match exact-model manufacturer values.

## Remaining limitations

Physical rings were verified from manufacturer instructions and parts labels; photos were not needed to resolve these Fujinon records. No assertion is made about ring clicks, de-click options, aperture-ring locks, or whether a control is mechanically coupled to the diaphragm. Those are distinct properties from the existing boolean.

Official product/specification pages were also fetched for all 48 candidate URLs. Several discontinued-model pages returned HTTP 403 or did not expose specifications; their manufacturer manuals were used instead. A blocked page was not counted as confirmation. The two weight corrections have matching manual and accessible product-page evidence. No unresolved weight or aperture-ring field remains among these 48 records; other fields retain the unverified scope stated above.
